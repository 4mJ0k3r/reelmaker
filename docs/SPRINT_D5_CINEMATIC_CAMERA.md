# Cinematic camera coverage

The 0.2 shot can use a hold, pan, track, push-in, pullback or reframe. Motion
uses bounded eased offsets over the authored 90-frame shot. Tracking
compositions follow sampled roots. The two-shot centers and scales the pair,
and medium, follow and detail shots constrain horizontal subject margins.
A close reaction places a left-facing character on the right side for look room.

An operator can select `auto` to derive coverage from the existing beat
order, actor action, previous framing and signal cue. This resolves to ordinary
registered camera presets before rendering. It places the environmental reveal
at its cue and ends a paired sequence in shared coverage. Explicit shots remain
available when a story needs more specific direction.

Outpost and ravine motion projects are under `examples/sprint-d`.
