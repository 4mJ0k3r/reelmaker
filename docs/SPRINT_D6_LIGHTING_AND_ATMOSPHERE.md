# Lighting and atmosphere

The frontier worlds have six trusted mood palettes with scene-authored skies,
ridge colors, terrain and atmosphere. The sunset and storm shelter uses
localized amber window glow and a spill on the ground. The ravine uses cold
mist, layered green firs and channel highlights, with higher actor readability
at night. The cinematic renderer mixes approved color treatments into actor
fills and strokes and deepens the contact shadow without adding pose paths.

Initial mobile views exposed a flat ravine horizon seam and snow-bright trees.
The final irregular terrain silhouette, haze band and green fir silhouettes
remove that clash. A decoded first-pass meeting also showed Bram's surprised
face holding after his posture had returned to neutral. The 0.2 acting pass
now coordinates the torso with the revised gesture timing, settles the face
and responds again at the lamp cue.

The visual evidence contains full and 360-wide previews, before/after
comparisons and a 90-frame storm project. Color treatments are authored
composites. They do not simulate a physical light transport model.
