## Actual refinements

The first final-demo decode exposed partial background partners in the arrival,
wait and ravine-bank compositions. Changes stay in the admitted interface:
shared two-shot/track for arrival and bank; environmental wait coverage; explicit
Mira focus on blackout instead of an automatically selected partner reaction.
The first output is preserved and a new final output was produced through the
clean operator CLI. The before/after sheet uses actual encoded frame samples.
All ten final decoded shot rows were inspected after this single refinement.

The direction discovery example moves Bram's anchor from 170 to 205 to avoid
edge cameos in reverse coverage. Short pacing examples use shared first-shot
coverage instead of a medium shot that could cut a non-focal partner. Refined
short clips are supplied for direct timing comparison. Styles change the
anticipation and speed of the same registered acting; they do not add gestures.
The director also varies redundant same-beat establishing coverage and repeated
same-subject reactions, while an explicit same-intent hold preserves deliberate
repetition. Explicit authored repetition is allowed and diagnosed.

## Story observations

The Last Signal clearly presents approach, companion, flash and blackout.
The Answering Light communicates isolation, encounter and returning light;
its two diagnostic cues do not constitute narration or a finished soundscape.
Before the Storm offers meaningful geographic/prop variation and a reunion,
but the creek-cart-to-village-parcel transfer and sunset are editorial ellipses.
The Last Transmission's practical-light events and visible fear/attention
changes work without captions; the survival stakes and radio/transmission
metaphor are not literal equipment operation. No invented button press,
unregistered physical interaction or automatic screenplay interpretation is claimed.

Remaining artistic limitations require review and future authored performance
work. They are not reasons to destabilize the rig or rewrite timing in this
release candidate. Final artistic acceptance remains with the independent reviewer.
